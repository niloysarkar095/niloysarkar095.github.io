Robbers - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Robbers ("the game", "we") uses, and why. The game has no accounts for you to create and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location.

Information stored on your device

Your progress - the cars you own, your bags of cash, your scores, the initials you enter on the leaderboard and your settings - is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Worldwide leaderboard

When you save a new best score, the game sends it to the worldwide leaderboard together with the three initials you chose, the car you drove, the bags you collected and the time of the run. To keep one best score per player, the game signs in anonymously and receives a random player identifier; it is not linked to your name, your Google account, your device's hardware or anything else about you. The game also downloads the leaderboard when it starts and after you set a new best. Your initials, score, bags and car are shown publicly to other players on the leaderboard.

The leaderboard runs on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes this data, including your IP address when the game connects, on our behalf; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs. Uninstalling the game removes the identifier from your device, but not your entry; to have your entry removed, contact us at the address below with your initials, score and roughly when you set it.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example to double the bags from your next run, or to continue a run). The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with the ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent, using Google's consent form, before ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time from Settings > PRIVACY OPTIONS. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Update check

When it starts, the game downloads a small file from this website to check whether your version is still supported. No information about you is sent in this request beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address), which the website host (GitHub Pages) may log as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

Robbers is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information. Apart from the services named above, we do not share it with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
